# Phase 10 — analytics and reports

Date: 14 October 2026. [Plan](PHASE10_PLAN.md), [gallery](SCREENSHOTS.md).

Implemented the roadmap's private analytics dashboard: actual completion/status and overdue statistics, planned workload/capacity, milestone progress, immutable sprint velocity, recorded activity trends, observed sprint burndown, custom UTC dates and six CSV datasets. Current tenant/resource permissions protect reports and downloads; guests/archives read only. No demonstration series or productivity score is added.

Current tasks and subtasks count as distinct work items; explicit project/milestone delivery status stays separate. Overdue means unfinished with dueDate before today in UTC. Forecast shares entered estimates across explicit assignees and prorates known schedules; capacity uses business days. Missing effort, schedule and capacity remain explicit. Task-change activity counts creation, edits, moves and deletion; all project changes include planning and other recorded activity. These counts are not completed-work or individual performance scores.

Completed sprint counts and known completed estimate hours come from frozen delivery snapshots, with unknown completed effort shown separately. They are not story points or logged hours. Old snapshots have unavailable effort. Burndown observations record actual changed active-sprint totals transactionally; completion records before carryover. Earlier history is blank, known values carry forward, and curves stop at completion/current time. Added, removed or reopened work can increase/decrease scope. Completed legacy sprints never fabricate a baseline. Observation failure rolls back the associated mutation.

CSV has consistent quoted columns, repeated project/time/window metadata, Unicode/newline support and formula neutralization. Empty datasets are header-only; unknown cells are blank. Reports/exports enforce resource bounds, current authority and no-store headers. Recharts 3.10.1/react-is 19.3.0 are pinned and lazy; charts support keyboard inspection, data tables, series/unit controls and reduced motion. [Official accessibility guidance](https://github.com/recharts/recharts/wiki/Recharts-and-accessibility).
